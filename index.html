About Antonia Tuition Center

Welcome to Antonia Tuition Center, a premier educational hub with a modern European academic outlook. We specialize in providing structured, high-quality tuition support for school students ranging from Class 1 to Class 10.

Our mission is to foster a deep understanding of core subjects, build unshakeable confidence, and develop critical problem-solving and coding skills tailored for modern young learners.

Middle Classes (6 - 8)

Advanced conceptual clarity in Mathematics, General Science, English, and analytical thinking.

High School (9 - 10)

Rigorous preparation for board/school exams with deep focus on Physics, Chemistry, and Advanced Math.

Introduction to basic coding logic, block programming, and computer fundamentals designed for young minds.
